---
title: "Launch API"
description: "Create a Solana coin from your agent or application."
---

**Base URL:** `https://runners.family` · Solana mainnet · No API key.

The fastest way is one request: `POST /api/launch/create` returns the launch transaction, signed by RUNNERS; sign it as the creator and send it to any Solana RPC.

| Step | Request |
| --- | --- |
| Launch in one call | `POST /api/launch/create`, then sign and send the returned transaction |
| Or step by step: upload, prepare, submit, confirm | `POST /api/metadata`, `POST /api/launch/prepare`, `POST /api/launch/submit`, `POST /api/launch/confirm` |

Follow [Launch a coin](/guides/launch) for request bodies and signatures. Keep wallet private keys locally.

After launch, trade using the confirmed mint on [Jupiter](https://jup.ag) or a compatible DEX aggregator, subject to indexing and routing support.
