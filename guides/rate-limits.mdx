---
title: "Rate limits"
description: "Respect request budgets, per-wallet quotas and retry instructions."
---

Limits apply per client address. Responses over a limit use HTTP `429`; honor `Retry-After` before retrying.

| Operation | Default per client |
| --- | --- |
| Launch preparation, submission and estimates | 30 per minute, burst 15 |
| Metadata uploads | 12 per 10 minutes, burst 6 |
| Launch confirmation | 60 per minute, burst 30 |

Limited responses carry `RateLimit-Limit`, `RateLimit-Remaining`, `RateLimit-Reset` and `RateLimit-Policy`. There is no integrator API-key tier. Operators can change these defaults.
