## Typography

The website uses **Geist**. The lowercase wordmark uses **Geist Semibold (600)** with tight spacing. Use the supplied artwork to keep its shape and spacing consistent.

## Usage

- Preserve proportions and leave clear space around the artwork.
- Place the logo on a contrasting, uncluttered background.
- Keep the supplied colors and shapes. Avoid stretching, rotating or adding effects.
- Write **RUNNERS** in prose; use the supplied lowercase wordmark in artwork.
- Do not imply official endorsement or a partnership without permission.
